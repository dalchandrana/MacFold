Apple Silicon Architecture

Engineering at the metal.

Built exclusively in native Swift 6 and Metal 3.0. Zero runtime dependencies, zero accounts, and zero cloud services.

Privacy First

Your screen stays on your Mac.

ScreenCaptureKit captures the desktop into bounded local GPU memory buffers only. Frames are never written to disk, never analyzed, and never sent over the network. Mac Fold automatically excludes its own windows from capture.

ScreenCaptureKit → Locked Bounded Frame Store → Metal 3.0 Shader → Display (0 Disk I/O, 0 Network)
1.94 ms

Metal 3.0 Pipeline

Sub-2.3ms 95th percentile fragment shader execution on Apple Silicon. Up to ProMotion 120Hz refresh rates supported.

30 Hz

IOKit LID Sensor

Direct low-latency reads from MacBook HID Sensor Page 0x20, Usage 0x8A. Runs on a dedicated asynchronous queue without blocking the main UI thread.

2.0 s

Lid Stillness Auto-Clear

Hold the lid still at any angle and the effect smoothly clears after 1–5 seconds (2s default). Settle down and keep working at any physical angle without disruption.

Hardware Verification

Check your MacBook compatibility.

Mac Fold requires a modern Apple Silicon MacBook equipped with the integrated lid-angle sensor.

Fast Setup

Up and running in sixty seconds.

Download the disk image, allow the first launch, and experience your desktop following your lid.

1

Download & Move

Download Mac-Fold.dmg, open it, and drag Mac Fold into your Applications folder.

Or grab the portable ZIP release if you prefer manual extraction.
2

Allow First Launch

As an open-source unnotarized app, macOS Gatekeeper may show a warning. Simply visit System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Follow Apple's official guide →
3

Grant Screen Recording

Click Enable Mac Fold. When prompted, allow Screen Recording so Metal shaders can locally render your desktop into bounded RAM.

Try Replay first — it tests all 6 effects without any permissions!
