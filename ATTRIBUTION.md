### Hardware Notes & Compatibility

Mac Fold interacts directly with the MacBook unibody lid-angle hardware sensor (HID Sensor Page `0x20`, Usage `0x8A`, Feature Report `1`) to detect real-time physical lid movement on compatible Apple Silicon MacBooks (M2/M3/M4 generation MacBooks with integrated lid-angle sensors).
