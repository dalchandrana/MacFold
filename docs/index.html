DUO / THE ORIGINAL EFFECTPREVIEW

Generated artwork demonstrating Duo. Your actual desktop is animated locally on your Mac.

SMALL APP. TANGIBLE MOVEMENT.

Move the lid.
Feel the difference.

01

It follows your hand.

The effect responds to the MacBook’s lid angle. Reverse direction and the display follows you back.

02

Hold still. See clearly.

Pause at any comfortable angle. After your chosen 1–5 seconds, the desktop and live preview return to normal.

03

Quiet when you are.

Settled previews stop rendering. Blur work is reused, with refresh limits that respond to power and temperature.

04

Your screen stays yours.

Desktop frames stay in memory on your Mac. No uploads, recordings, accounts, or analytics.

THE NEXT FOUR

More ways to move.

Duo is available now. Four original mechanical effects are in development.

01 / ROLL

A flexible sheet.
A rolling hinge.

The desktop curls down toward the hinge, with wrapped pixels and a moving highlight.

02 / SHUTTER

Four panels.
One smooth close.

Rigid panels slide behind one another, with overlapping edges and contact shadows.

03 / FLEX

A little tension.
A gentle bend.

A continuous display bows inward before settling back into a perfectly flat surface.

04 / IRIS

Precision blades.
A closing aperture.

Dark blades overlap around the desktop in a restrained mechanical twist.
